# Chamber Heater

Custom Rat Rig chamber-heater and fan-adapter CAD.

## Files

- `Rat Rig Chamber Heater.step` - original chamber heater STEP reference.
- `Rat Rig Vcore 4 Chamber Heater.step` - smaller Rat Rig V-Core 4 chamber heater variant.
- `Noctua 140mm Fan adapter.step` - 140 mm Noctua fan adapter STEP reference.

## Fitment And Safety

Review heater placement, wiring, fan direction, material choice, and chamber-temperature limits before printing or installing. These files are unofficial custom build references and should be checked against the exact printer revision and enclosure setup.
